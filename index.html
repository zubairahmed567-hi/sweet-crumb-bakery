Our Story

Crafted with Passion, Served with Love

Sweet Crumbs Bakery was born in 2016 from a simple dream — to bring the warmth of a home kitchen to every corner of Karachi. What started as weekend pastries for neighbours grew into one of the city's most beloved artisan bakeries.

Every product is handmade by our passionate bakers using locally sourced ingredients and time‑honoured recipes. No shortcuts. No preservatives. Just pure, honest baking.
