How It All Began

From a Home Kitchen to Karachi's Favourite

Sweet Crumbs Bakery was founded in 2016 by Nadia Ahmed, a passionate home baker who had spent years perfecting recipes handed down by her grandmother. What began as weekend baking sessions for friends and family quickly turned into something far bigger.

Word spread fast. Neighbours were knocking at her door, coworkers started placing orders, and soon her tiny kitchen couldn't keep up. In 2016, Nadia opened the first Sweet Crumbs storefront in Bahadurabad, Karachi.

Today, Sweet Crumbs employs 22 passionate bakers and serves hundreds of customers daily. But our promise remains unchanged: every item is made by hand, fresh every morning, using the finest ingredients.

Our Process

From Ingredients to Art

Our bakers arrive at 4 AM each morning to begin preparing the day's fresh batches. Everything starts from scratch — flour is measured, butter is softened, fruit is sliced and flavours are carefully balanced to achieve our signature taste.

By the time our shop opens at 8 AM, the aroma of fresh baking already fills the neighbourhood. No frozen ingredients. No pre‑made mixes. Just pure, honest craftsmanship in every single bite.
