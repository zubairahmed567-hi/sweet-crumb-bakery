4 AM We Start Baking
Our Process

From Ingredients to Art

Our bakers arrive at 4 AM each morning to begin preparing the day's fresh batches. Everything starts from scratch — flour is measured, butter is softened, fruit is sliced, flavours are balanced to achieve our signature taste.

By the time our shop opens at 8 AM, the aroma of fresh baking already fills the neighbourhood. No frozen ingredients. No pre‑made mixes. Just pure, honest craftsmanship in every single bite.
