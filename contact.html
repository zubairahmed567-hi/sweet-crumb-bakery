Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order a custom cake?

We recommend ordering at least 3–5 days in advance for standard custom cakes, and 1–2 weeks for elaborate wedding or tiered cakes to ensure the best quality.

Do you offer delivery across all of Karachi?

Yes! We deliver to all major areas in Karachi. Delivery is free for orders above PKR 3,000. Standard delivery charges apply for smaller orders.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions or allergies?

Absolutely. We offer eggless, nut-free, and reduced-sugar options. Please mention your dietary requirements in the special instructions when ordering.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery, bank transfer, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, and all major debit/credit cards. A 50% advance is required for custom orders.
